## Art-direction review

- The wallpaper is abstract night/lace/candlelight artwork. It does **not**
  depict a deity, altar, possession scene, specific living person, text, QR code,
  logo, or third-party mark.
- The icon and mark shapes are geometric linework, not sacred or third-party
  symbols. No GrapheneOS, Android, Google Play, or Pixel logo is used.
- An external reviewer from the relevant communities remains a **release gate**
  before any public distribution. This record does not claim that review has
  happened.
- No third-party image, photograph, or scraped artwork is included. The vector
  sources are committed so the artwork can be audited and modified. A rights
  review of any AI-assisted design workflow remains a release gate.
